Try Iris on the Balance 2

A pretend watch you can actually use, with made-up answers. It works like the real app: dictation, a pinned chat, history, and animated eyes while Iris works. Turn the crown with your mouse wheel. On the right you can see what travels between watch, phone and home.

What to try

  1. Ask Iris starts a new chat with the watch's dictation, then ✓. The eyes show what Iris is doing; read the reply with the crown, with small eyes on top.
  2. Continue (at the end of the reply) dictates again in the same conversation; New chat starts a new one.
  3. History (the round clock button next to the pinned chat): tap a conversation to continue it, or Pin to put it on the "Pinned chat" button (just one).
  4. Pick the next question to "dictate" below.

How it works

Full-screen eyes while you waitBig eyes and what Iris is doing. Every 2 seconds the phone asks Hermes for the run's status and the tool in use. If it takes longer than about 5 minutes, "Keep waiting" resumes the same answer.
